# T67 — the two-factor Hull-White model (G2++)

**Cowork spec, 2026-11-20.** Second tranche of the single-curve short-rate arc (project nine,
`MCINERNEY-ZASTAWNIAK`; `docs/stochastic_rates_arc.md`). Two correlated Gaussian factors instead of one —
the first model in the tree with a **factor correlation** in its own dynamics, so the T17/T43 correlation
discipline from the spread arc (T63) returns, and the new §4 finding-2 clause gets its first real use (the
two-factor bond variance carries `(1−e^{−(a+b)τ})/(a+b)`-type cross terms with removable singularities).

## Target
Price a zero-coupon bond and a caplet/swaption under the two-factor Hull-White model (G2++) —
`price(product, G2ppModel) → PricingResult` — with `r(t) = x(t) + y(t) + φ(t)`, `x` and `y` correlated OU
factors and `φ` the deterministic shift that fits the initial curve.

## Pieces, layer, reuse
- **The model** (new, L3) — `G2ppModel` carrying the `MarketSnapshot` (§3) and the parameters: the two
  mean-reversion speeds `(a, b)`, the two vols `(σ_x, σ_y)`, the correlation `ρ`, and the deterministic `φ`.
  Over the field limit (§7) → **bundle** into a frozen value object (`G2ppParams` or a two-`HullWhiteParams`
  + `ρ` composite — Code rules the shape; the correlation is what makes it one object, not two). **Distinct
  from `HullWhiteModel` and `VasicekModel`** — a two-factor correlated model is a different idea; no merge
  (the §6 call, the T60/T66 precedent).
- **The affine bond** (new, L3) — `P(t,T) = A(t,T)·e^{−B_x(t,T)·x − B_y(t,T)·y}`, where **`B_x = hw_beta(a,
  t, T)` and `B_y = hw_beta(b, t, T)`** — reuse `hw_beta` for both, do not re-derive. `A(t,T)` carries the
  variance term **including the `ρ`-weighted cross term** `(2ρσ_xσ_y/ab)·[...]` — the genuinely-new formula.
- **The caplet/swaption** (new engine, L4) — Gaussian factors, so the bond is lognormal and the option is
  Black on the forward bond with the two-factor bond variance; reuse `black_price` and the
  `bond_option_variance`/`zero_bond_option` pattern where the variance structure matches, else the new
  two-factor variance.
- **The MC companion** reuses **`simulate_lgm_states`** per factor (each is OU) and **`pseudo_square_root`**
  (`foundation/linalg.py`) to correlate the two factor increments — the T63 correlated-simulation reuse.

## The mathematics (re-transcribe from the source — NOT this spec, §5)
Read **McInerney–Zastawniak ch 3.7 (two-factor Hull-White)** in `-raw` and `-layout` and reconcile (§4).
The load-bearing new formula is the **bond variance's cross term** — the `ρ`-weighted
`(1−e^{−(a+b)(T−t)})/(a+b)` piece — and `A(t,T)`. Transcribe the source's **own parameterisation** (the α/θ
collision at T66 is the warning: do not substitute a familiar form). The spec names the obligation, not the
form (§4).

## Oracles (RED-first, §4)
1. **Pointwise transcription of `A(t,T)` and the bond variance cross term.** `B_x`/`B_y` are `hw_beta`, so
   **cross-check them against `hw_beta`** (same object — consistency guard, not independence, §4 caveat). The
   cross term is the new load-bearing formula and gets its own pointwise transcription.
2. **The correlation is pinned TWO ways, neither substituting for the other (T63 discipline).** `ρ` enters
   the bond variance as the **product** `ρσ_xσ_y`, so (a) assert the **value** of the cross term against an
   independent transcription at non-trivial `ρ` (T43 — presence/sign is not magnitude), and (b) pin `ρ`
   **directly observable** in the MC: the **realised covariance** of the two simulated factor increments
   reproduces `σ_xσ_yρ·dt` (T17 — a price pins only the ρ-weighted scalar; a factorisation bias shows as
   failure-to-improve as √N). Prove the simulated correlation **unbiased before** crediting the price band
   (T19).
3. **Reduction: σ_y → 0 ⇒ one-factor Hull-White** (exact — the second factor vanishes, `r = x + φ`).
   **Companion required (§4/T39):** a genuine two-factor case (both vols non-zero, `ρ ≠ 0`) checked against
   the pointwise transcription — the reduction kills the second factor and the correlation, so it is blind to
   exactly what the model adds. State the premise.
4. **The new finding-2 clause applies here — its first real use.** The cross term
   `(1−e^{−(a+b)τ})/(a+b)` is `0/0` as `a+b → 0` (and each `hw_beta` is `0/0` as its own speed → 0), so near
   those limits catastrophic cancellation makes the closed form diverge from its limit. **Do NOT band the
   `a→0` or `a+b→0` reductions** — assert them as an **O(·) convergence rate over the regime where the
   closed form is numerically sound**, with a companion stating where it degrades (the T66 clause verbatim;
   this arc's reason the clause exists).
5. **Requirement oracles:** the bond boundary `P(T,T) = 1` exactly; the `ρ = ±1` degenerate limits (perfect
   correlation collapses the two-factor variance to a one-factor form — assert the closed form); put–call
   parity on the caplet.
6. **Cross-check against the one-factor Hull-White** at `σ_y = 0`: the G2++ bond and the tree's
   `HullWhiteModel` bond must agree exactly (the reuse made honest — consistency guard, §4 same-object
   caveat).

## Degeneracy premises (§4 / §9)
- **`ρ ≠ 0`, `σ_x ≠ σ_y`, and `a ≠ b`** in the main fixtures — at `ρ = 0` the cross term vanishes (a dropped
  `ρσ_xσ_y` survives), at `a = b` the two `hw_beta` coincide (the two factors become indistinguishable). Assert
  the premise; prefer a **mutation to a reading**: drop the cross term, flip `ρ`'s sign, `ρ → ρ²`, drop the
  `2` or the `1/ab` in the cross term, swap `a`/`b`.
- The `σ_y → 0` companion is **non-degenerate in the second factor** (T39).
- The realised-covariance pin needs enough paths that a factorisation bias is resolvable above the SE (T17).
